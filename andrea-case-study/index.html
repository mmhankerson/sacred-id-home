Sacred Healing Journeys · Case Study

From technology overwhelm to a business built to grow.

How Andrea created an online home for her healing work, courses, and next chapter.

The shift

A website that clients can find, trust, and use.

Built to support

Healing sessions, online booking, courses, and continued growth.

01 · Starting point

A growing vision held back by technology.

When Andrea began working with Matt, she was transitioning from full-time high school teaching toward a business that brought together her healing work and experience as an educator.

She had already created two websites, but neither could support the business she was beginning to envision. They were basic, difficult to update, and lacked online scheduling and course capabilities. Andrea knew she needed something more professional, but she did not know how to make the technology work.

“I was trying to figure out how to get a website created that also included the ability for clients to book sessions with me online, as well as a way for me to teach courses through an online platform.”
02 · The challenge

Finding someone who understood the technology and the work.

Technology was only part of the problem.

Andrea’s previous website had been created by someone who did not understand spiritual work. Content was added that did not make sense for her business, and requesting corrections became a slow, frustrating process.

“It became a nightmare. He would put things on my site that didn’t make any sense in relation to the work I was doing, and then it would take weeks, if not months, to get things changed. It was embarrassing.”

Andrea needed more than a web designer. She needed someone who could understand the meaning behind her work, communicate it clearly, and build the systems required to support her services and courses. Meeting Matt at a spiritual show in Mankato gave her the connection she had been looking for.

03 · The solution

A website that felt like her and worked for her clients.

Matt helped Andrea create a professional website that communicated her work clearly while preserving the gentle, welcoming feeling she wanted clients to experience.

The new site gave visitors a simple way to:

  • Understand Andrea’s healing work
  • Explore her services and courses
  • Book sessions online
  • Register for educational offerings
  • Navigate the site without confusion
“Early on, I really felt understood. Matt helped design a site that clearly communicates the work I offer, while also reflecting the soft image and welcoming feeling I want people to experience when they visit my site. It’s also very easy to navigate.”

The result was not simply a better-looking website. Andrea finally had the practical foundation she needed to expand her work.

04 · The result

New possibilities for sessions and courses.

Over the following year, Andrea’s website became an active part of her business. Clients could find her online, schedule sessions themselves, and enroll in courses. Andrea was able to offer experiences she previously did not know how to deliver through technology.

“The website was monumental for my growth. Over the last year, I’ve been able to do so much more because my clients can find my site, book an online session, or sign up for a course.”
“This has been huge for my expansion. It has allowed me to do things and offer courses that I never imagined I’d be able to figure out how to do.”
The clearest result

Instead of technology limiting what Andrea could offer, her website began helping her put more of her vision into practice.

05 · The next phase

A roadmap for continued growth.

As Andrea’s business evolved, Matt helped her look beyond the original website and consider what the next stage required.

Through strategic conversations, an Akashic Records reading, and a focused roadmap, they identified opportunities to streamline her systems, improve the website, and break larger goals into manageable steps.

“The roadmap is helping me see where I need to streamline my systems and make a few changes to the site. It’s giving me a scaffolded, clear idea of how I can continue to expand and grow.”

Andrea began placing projects on her calendar and dividing them into smaller actions so they would feel less overwhelming. Instead of carrying several possibilities at once, she had a structure for moving forward.

“It helped me see areas where I still need growth, as well as some of my blind spots and healing I still need to do. It also gave me comfort around my dad’s passing and helped me not be so hard on myself.”
06 · The deeper change

A clearer direction and an ongoing partnership.

Andrea now feels more confident about the direction of her business and more capable of following through on her ideas.

“I feel like I have a much clearer direction because of the guidance I’ve been given by Matt.”

For Andrea, the relationship did not end when the website launched. As her vision expanded, she continued to have someone who understood both the practical demands of building a business and the nature of spiritual work.

“I would tell other practitioners that Matt is not only thorough, accessible, and highly knowledgeable, but he has the added bonus of getting it—meaning spiritual work. He truly understands how to explain what this work is about and incorporate it into a website in a way that makes sense.”
“He’s also easygoing, kind, and professional, and he makes people feel really comfortable throughout the entire process—from beginning to end, or really, ongoing. There isn’t necessarily an ‘end’ to the relationship just because the website is built.”
07 · What it demonstrates

A digital foundation should make more possible.

Andrea did not need technology for technology’s sake. She needed a digital foundation that could help her bring more of her work into the world.

By combining clear messaging, thoughtful design, practical systems, and ongoing strategy, she moved from feeling limited by technology to having a website that supports sessions, courses, and continued growth.
